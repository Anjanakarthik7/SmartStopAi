AI-Based Smart Passenger Monitoring & Destination Alert System

A novel smart transit architecture synchronizing IoT edge sensor arrays, machine learning destination inference, and multi-modal pre-arrival alert delivery — prototype validated on the Namma Metro network.

IPC: B61L 25/00 IPC: G08G 1/00 CPC: G06Q 50/40 Lovely Professional University Complete Patent Filing

Patent Claims (1 – 7)

  • 1
    System for real-time intelligent passenger monitoring with AI inference engine predicting individual destination stops.
  • 2
    AI engine applying personalised travel history (Bayesian inference) to produce destination probability scores.
  • 3
    Smart Stop Alert Module delivering multi-modal notifications — push, voice TTS, and haptic feedback.
  • 4
    Elderly Assistance Mode with enlarged UI, voice guidance, and caregiver notification relay.
  • 5
    SOS Emergency Module enabling real-time passenger-to-operator communication with GPS logging.
  • 6
    Driver/Operator Fleet Dashboard rendering real-time disembarkation demand analytics per stop.
  • 7
    Method for AI-based destination prediction: collect boarding data → Bayesian inference → confidence score → pre-arrival alert.
